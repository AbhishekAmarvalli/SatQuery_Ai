02 · Architecture

From question to grounded map answer

The proposed system architecture from our SIH proposal — seven steps, one workflow.

  1. 1

    User query

    Natural-language question, e.g. “Show areas affected by flooding in this region.”

  2. 2

    Multimodal data

    Optical, SAR & Thermal satellite imagery is selected for the AOI.

  3. 3

    Vision-language AI

    LLaVA / GEOCHAT interprets the image together with your query.

  4. 4

    Geospatial grounding

    SAM / CLIP-BLIP detects and localises the relevant regions (mask).

  5. 5

    Backend & DB

    FastAPI orchestrates models; PostgreSQL + PostGIS stores imagery & spatial results.

  6. 6

    Interactive map

    React + Leaflet / OpenLayers renders the map, layers, zoom, pan & highlights.

  7. 7

    Grounded output

    Highlighted regions + AI insight, e.g. “red areas indicate flooding from optical, SAR & thermal analysis.”

Key challenges & mitigations

Very large imagesImage tiling / sliding-window inference
High compute costModel quantisation + GPU/cloud inference
3 different modalitiesModality-specific preprocessing & fine-tuning
Grounding accuracySAM / CLIP-BLIP verification of regions

03 · Free public data

Three sensors, one question box

Every dataset below is free & public — no API keys, no billing. The assistant picks the sensor that can actually answer your question.

OPTICAL

Change & description

“What changed since 2017?”, “Describe the land cover”, “How much vegetation is left?”

  • Sentinel-2 true colour, 10–60 m
  • Reflectance you can see — best for before/after
  • Cloud-limited: needs clear-sky passes

source: Copernicus S2 · EOX s2cloudless · free

THERMAL

Floods & heat

“Where are the floods?”, “Which areas are hot?”, “Show drought stress.”

  • MODIS land-surface temperature, 1 km
  • Standing water & soil moisture show up as cold anomalies
  • Heat islands, fire risk & drought signals

source: NASA EOSDIS GIBS · free & open

SAR

Night & all-weather

“Show port activity at night”, “Ships in the dark”, “Anything through the clouds?”

  • Sentinel-1 C-band radar, 20 m
  • Illuminates itself — works at night & through clouds
  • Ports, ships, structures & water surfaces pop

source: Copernicus S1 · Planetary Computer · free

MVP note: routing runs on a real vision-language model (Groq / Gemini / OpenAI-compatible, see .env.example) that receives your AOI imagery and returns the sensor choice, the answer and its regions; those regions are clamped to your AOI inside the free public zone.
